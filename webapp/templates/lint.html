The linters

Quick checks (QS)
Errors Python or Qiskit would raise, read from the source: syntax, a missing circuit, indices outside the registers, removed, deprecated or unknown Qiskit APIs, and operations Qiskit rejects while the static parser rebuilds the circuit.
Dependence checks (QD)
Quantum-side mistakes, read from the rebuilt circuit and its dependence graph: gates no measurement depends on, measurements overwritten or constant, controls never set, idle qubits, cancelling pairs, gates with no effect.
LintQ (LQ)
A port of the LintQ analyses to the slicer's static front end.
Where they run
On every Analyze in the Slicer, where a finding marks its nodes in the graph, and on every bug of a Benchmark dataset.
